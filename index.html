WHO WE ARE

AeroBMSCE is BMS College of Engineering’s official aerospace and UAV innovation club. Since 2012, we have brought together students from 10+ departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft and autonomous systems.

WHAT WE DO

We design competitive aircraft and UAVs, explore drone racing and autonomy, and develop fabrication and testing expertise through hands-on projects that turn engineering concepts into real-world solutions.

WHY IT MATTERS

We equip students with practical engineering skills, real-world problem-solving experience, and industry exposure while creating opportunities for brands to connect with emerging talent in aerospace and technology.

OUR VISION

We aspire to become a leading aerospace and UAV research group, recognised for innovation, design excellence, and competitive success while inspiring the next generation of aerospace engineers. worldwide.
